# Mission, domain, and teaching frame established

Rodrigo confirmed all four motivations at once — apply the practices in his own Java work, teach and evangelise them with evidence, author his own skills on the same principles, and hold the full intellectual genealogy. That breadth is the reason the curriculum runs four tracks rather than one: no single track serves all four, and dropping any track drops a stated goal.

Three decisions that bind every future session:

- **Running domain is banking** (`Account`, `Transfer`, `Balance`, `Ledger`). Chosen for strong invariants. Every lesson extends this same domain — the familiarity is what buys attention for the new concept.
- **pt-BR prose, English technical terms verbatim.** Never translate a term of art.
- **Evidence bar is unusually high.** He asked for *evidências reais*, which is why the four-tier marking system (`Quoted` / `Metadata verified` / `Chapter located` / `[unverified]`) exists and why coinages are labelled as coinages.

**Implications for teaching:** the "evangelise" goal means every claim must survive being repeated to a skeptical third party — so negative empirical results get reported alongside favourable ones, and our own analogies are marked as ours. The "author my own skills" goal means Track IV is not optional colour; it is a deliverable, and lesson 0020 is the real finish line.

**Evidence:** stated directly in answers to a four-question scoping prompt at the start of session 1 (2026-07-31). Not yet corroborated by observed work — no lesson has been completed at time of writing, so this record captures *stated* intent, not demonstrated understanding. See [[MISSION.md]].
